# ILM AI — علم سب کے لیے
### Real-time Urdu Subtitles for YouTube

ILM AI is a free Chrome extension that automatically translates YouTube subtitles into Urdu in real-time — helping Pakistani students access English educational content in their native language.

---

## What it does
- Detects English captions on any YouTube video
- Translates them to Urdu instantly using AI
- Displays Urdu subtitles directly on the video screen
- Works on any educational channel (Khan Academy, MIT, 3Blue1Brown, and more)

---

## Installation (5 minutes, completely free)

### Step 1 — Download the extension
Click the green **Code** button on this page → Click **Download ZIP** → Extract the ZIP file on your computer

### Step 2 — Open Chrome Extensions
Open Google Chrome and type this in the address bar: chrome://extensions/

### Step 3 — Enable Developer Mode
In the top right corner of the extensions page, toggle **Developer Mode** ON

### Step 4 — Load the extension
Click **Load Unpacked** → Select the **ILM AI** folder you extracted in Step 1

### Step 5 — Start watching
Go to any YouTube educational video → Click the **CC button** to turn on English subtitles → Urdu subtitles will appear automatically!

---

## How to use
1. Go to any YouTube video that has English captions
2. Click the CC (closed captions) button on the video
3. ILM AI will automatically show Urdu subtitles on screen

---

## Built for Pakistani students
Pakistan has millions of students who want to access world-class education on YouTube but face a language barrier, as most schools are unable to teach the english language. ILM AI bridges that gap — for free, forever.
